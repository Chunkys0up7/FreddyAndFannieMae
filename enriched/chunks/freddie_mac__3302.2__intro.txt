# 3302.2: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/3302.2 -->

(a)

#### Damage or loss of Mortgage files

The Servicer must bear the entire cost of promptly restoring Mortgage files and related documents and records damaged or lost for any reason.

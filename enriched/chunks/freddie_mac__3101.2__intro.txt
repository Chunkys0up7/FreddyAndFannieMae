# 3101.2: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/3101.2 -->

(a)

#### Avoiding employing Named Parties

Seller/Servicers must establish and maintain procedures to ensure Seller/Servicers do not employ or contract with Named Parties for any purpose directly related to the origination, underwriting, or Servicing of a Freddie Mac Mortgage, subject to any conditions or exclusions in the applicable SCP Named Party’s final suspension order.

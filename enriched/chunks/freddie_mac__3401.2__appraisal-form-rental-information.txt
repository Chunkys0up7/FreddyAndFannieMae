#### Appraisal form rental information

Rental information on completed appraisal forms (i.e., [Form 1000, Single Family Comparable Rent Schedule](/app/guide/form/1000), or [Form 72, Small Residential Income Property Appraisal Report](/app/guide/form/72), as applicable), in accordance with the requirements of [Chapter 5306](/app/guide/chapter/5306), must be included in the Mortgage file.

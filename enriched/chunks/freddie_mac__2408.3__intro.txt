# 2408.3: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/2408.3 -->

(a)

#### Special representations and warranties, covenants, and agreements

(i)

##### Compliance and commitments in Servicing Freddie Mac Mortgages

The Servicer represents and warrants to, covenants and agrees with Freddie Mac that the Servicer is in compliance with and will continue to comply with all of the requirements of this chapter, any other applicable Purchase Documents, system processes and rules, terms of use, privacy policy, security standards and any user guides and instructions in connection with Servicing Freddie Mac Mortgages using the Servicing Tools.

(ii)

##### Controlled access to the Freddie Mac Exclusionary List

The Freddie Mac Exclusionary List is made available to Servicers via Freddie Mac Gateway®. Each time the Servicer accesses any Freddie Mac Servicing Tool, the Servicer represents and warrants to Freddie Mac that Servicer has never:

- Provided access to or otherwise made the Exclusionary List available to a Related Third Party or any other party except as permitted under and in accordance with [Section 1201.8(a)(iii)](/app/guide/section/1201.8#a_iii); or
- Enabled or permitted any person or entity on the Exclusionary List to access or use any Servicing Tool whether directly or indirectly through the Servicer or any Related Third Party

Further, the Servicer covenants and agrees that it will not, under any circumstances, do so in the future.

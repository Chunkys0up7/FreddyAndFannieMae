## Documenting reviews


Effective 03/02/2016

The Seller must:

- Maintain complete records for each Mortgage file selected for a preclosing or a post-closing quality control review
- Document and explain discrepancies or inconsistencies found in the Mortgage file that affect the eligibility of the Mortgage based on the requirements of the Seller, the MI or Freddie Mac

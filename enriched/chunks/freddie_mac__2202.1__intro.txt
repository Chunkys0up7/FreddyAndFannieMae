# 2202.1: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/2202.1 -->

(a)

#### Document Custodian engagement

For each Seller/Servicer number, the Seller/Servicer must contract with at least one Document Custodian, which may be a Designated Custodian or another approved Document Custodian chosen by the Seller/Servicer, on the Tri-Party Agreement, in accordance with the requirements of [Sections 2202.1(b)](#b) and [2202.2](2202.2).

(i)

##### Tri-Party Agreement limits

Seller/Servicers may enter into Tri-Party Agreements with up to 10 Document Custodians per Seller/Servicer number. Compensation for each Document Custodian's services is the sole responsibility of the Seller/Servicer.

(ii)

##### eMortgage requirements

For requirements related to eMortgages, see [Chapter 1402](/app/guide/chapter/1402).

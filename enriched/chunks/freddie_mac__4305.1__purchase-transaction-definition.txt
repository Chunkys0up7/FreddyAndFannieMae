#### Purchase transaction definition

A purchase transaction Mortgage is a Mortgage for which the proceeds are used for one of the following:

- Acquire the Mortgaged Premises
- Acquire the Mortgaged Premises and finance improvements to the property as permitted under the Seller’s Purchase Documents
- Pay off the Interim Construction Financing in accordance with the requirements of [Chapter 4602](/app/guide/chapter/4602) for Construction to Permanent Mortgages and Renovation Mortgages
- Pay off the outstanding balance under a land contract or contract for deed in accordance with requirements of [Chapter 4404](/app/guide/chapter/4404)

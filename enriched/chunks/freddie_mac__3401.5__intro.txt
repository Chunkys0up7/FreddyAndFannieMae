# 3401.5: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/3401.5 -->

(a)

#### Mortgages secured by Manufactured Homes

For a Mortgage secured by a Manufactured Home, the Mortgage file must include documentation evidencing that the Manufactured Home is legally classified as real property, the Manufactured Home is properly titled and the lien on the Manufactured Home is properly created, evidenced and perfected.

For a Mortgage on a new Manufactured Home, the Mortgage file must contain a copy of the manufacturer’s invoice and Manufactured Home Purchase Agreement in the Mortgage file.

If the Manufactured Home is a new installation, the Mortgage file must contain evidence that the Manufactured Home was installed in compliance with applicable HUD Codes for Manufactured Homes (HUD Codes).

# 3602.4: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/3602.4 -->

(a)

#### Survival of Freddie Mac's remedies; misrepresentations by the Seller or Servicer

Freddie Mac's decision to require or allow a repurchase in no way diminishes its right to pursue further action such as disqualification, suspension or termination, in whole or in part, of the Servicing Contract and related Servicing Contract Rights under the provisions of [Chapter 2301](/app/guide/chapter/2301) and/or [3603](/app/guide/chapter/3603). Freddie Mac may also exercise these remedies when its inspection of the documentation of a repurchased Mortgage reveals facts materially different from those for which Freddie Mac originally approved this repurchase.

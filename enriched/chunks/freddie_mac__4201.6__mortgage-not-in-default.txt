## Mortgage not in default


Effective 04/02/2025


[Guide Home](/app/guide/)

[Selling](/app/guide/segment/Selling)
[Series 4000 : Mortgage Eligibility](/app/guide/series/4000)
[Topic 4200 : General Mortgage Eligibility](/app/guide/topic/4200)
[Chapter 4201 : General Mortgage Eligibility](/app/guide/chapter/4201)

  Copy Link](#)
- [*print*
  Print](#)

As of the Freddie Mac Funding Date:

- No part of the Borrower’s monthly installment of principal and interest (and, if applicable, monthly Escrow payment) may have been 30 days or more delinquent (see definition of Delinquency in the [Glossary](/app/guide/glossary))
- There may not have been any other default under the terms and conditions of the Mortgage that remained uncured for 30 days or more after notice of the default to the Borrower

Before the Delivery Date, the Seller, or any prior holder of the Mortgage, must not have, directly or indirectly, advanced any funds to be used by or on behalf of the Borrower for the payment of principal, interest or other charge payable under the terms of the Mortgage.

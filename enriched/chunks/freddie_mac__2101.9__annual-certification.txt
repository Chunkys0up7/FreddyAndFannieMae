#### Annual certification

The Seller/Servicer must certify compliance with Freddie Mac’s requirements for fidelity and mortgagee’s E&O insurance on its Annual Certification Report, submitted in accordance with [Sections 2101.10](2101.10) and [2101.11](2101.11).

## Mortgage previously sold to the Government National Mortgage Association (GNMA)


Effective 04/02/2025


[Guide Home](/app/guide/)

[Selling](/app/guide/segment/Selling)
[Series 4000 : Mortgage Eligibility](/app/guide/series/4000)
[Topic 4200 : General Mortgage Eligibility](/app/guide/topic/4200)
[Chapter 4205 : Government funded, guaranteed or insured Mortgages](/app/guide/chapter/4205)


- [*copy url*
  Copy Link](#)
- [*print*
  Print](#)

A Mortgage previously sold to the Government National Mortgage Association (GNMA) and repurchased from GNMA, or a Mortgage on which a GNMA price differential has been paid, is eligible for sale to Freddie Mac, provided it meets all of the requirements of the Purchase Documents at the time it is offered for sale to Freddie Mac.

#### Leasehold Mortgage

If the Mortgaged Premises is on a leasehold estate, a copy of the lease with recordation information must be retained in the Mortgage file.

(j)

#### Buydown agreement

For Mortgages with a temporary subsidy buydown plan, the Mortgage file must contain a copy of the executed buydown agreement.

# 2201.2: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/2201.2 -->

(a)

#### Maintaining wire transfer instructions

In order to make timely and accurate wire transfers of cash proceeds for the purchase of Mortgages, Freddie Mac maintains a centralized database containing individual wire instructions as provided by the Seller on [Form 483](/app/guide/form/483).

This file is maintained and controlled in the Freddie Mac office in McLean, Virginia.

# 2203.1: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/2203.1 -->

(a)

#### MERS membership

A Seller/Servicer must comply with the requirements of the MERS Governing Documents if the Seller/Servicer is a MERS Member and sells to and/or services on behalf of Freddie Mac, as applicable, Mortgages registered on the MERS System.

If any requirements of the MERS Governing Documents conflict with the requirements of the Guide, the Seller/Servicer must comply with the requirements of the Guide.

Regardless of the eNote Vault System used by the eMortgage Servicing Agent, the Master Servicer and the eMortgage Servicing Agent must have obtained Freddie Mac’s approval of their fully executed [Form 479A, Single-Family Servicing Agent Certification and Agreement](/app/guide/form/479A), with an effective date on or after September 18, 2018.

For a Master Servicer and its eMortgage Servicing Agent that have been approved by Freddie Mac based on their submission of [Form 479A](/app/guide/form/479A) with an effective date prior to September 18, 2018, the Master Servicer and its eMortgage Servicing Agent must execute a new [Form 479A](/app/guide/form/479A) and submit it to Freddie Mac and obtain Freddie Mac’s prior approval in accordance with [Section 8102.1](8102.1).

Consistent with [Section 8201.1](8201.1), a Master Servicer may use only one eMortgage Servicing Agent for eMortgages being serviced under a particular Freddie Mac Seller/Servicer number at any one time. Freddie Mac’s approval of an eMortgage Servicing Agent is limited to the Master Servicer designated on the approved [Form 479A](/app/guide/form/479A). Before an eMortgage Servicing Agent can subservice eMortgages for another Master Servicer, a separate fully executed [Form 479A](/app/guide/form/479A) must be submitted to obtain Freddie Mac’s prior written approval.

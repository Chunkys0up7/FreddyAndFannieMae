## Assumption of Mortgage


Effective 04/02/2025


[Guide Home](/app/guide/)

[Selling](/app/guide/segment/Selling)
[Series 4000 : Mortgage Eligibility](/app/guide/series/4000)
[Topic 4200 : General Mortgage Eligibility](/app/guide/topic/4200)
[Chapter 4201 : General Mortgage Eligibility](/app/guide/chapter/4201)

  Copy Link](#)
- [*print*
  Print](#)

A Mortgage not owned by Freddie Mac with respect to which the original Borrower has conveyed the Mortgaged Premises to a new owner is eligible for purchase by Freddie Mac provided that the new owner:

- Is obligated by a written assumption agreement to repay the indebtedness secured by the Mortgaged Premises, and
- Has been fully underwritten and qualified according to the requirements of [Topics 5100 through 5500](/app/guide/topic/5100) and any other Guide sections applicable to the Mortgage being assumed. For example, if the Mortgaged Premises is a Manufactured Home, the provisions of [Chapter 5703](/app/guide/chapter/5703) must be satisfied.

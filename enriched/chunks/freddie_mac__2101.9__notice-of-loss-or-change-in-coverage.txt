#### Notice of loss or change in coverage

The Seller/Servicer must report to Freddie Mac the following events within 10 Business Days of their occurrence using the Change and Activity Report, submitted in accordance with [Section 2101.12](2101.12):

- The determination that any single act of embezzlement, theft of funds or fraud or mortgagee’s E&O loss has caused loss exceeding $100,000, whether or not Freddie Mac’s interests are affected or a claim is filed with the insurer, or
- The receipt of a notice from the insurer that the insurer has taken or intends to take action to cancel, reduce, not renew or restrictively modify the Seller/Servicer’s fidelity and/or mortgagee’s E&O insurance for any reason. The Seller/Servicer must include a copy of the insurer’s notice and detail the reasons for the insurer’s action or intended action if not stated in the insurer’s notice. The Seller/Servicer must also report its effort to obtain replacement coverage or otherwise satisfy Freddie Mac’s insurance requirements.

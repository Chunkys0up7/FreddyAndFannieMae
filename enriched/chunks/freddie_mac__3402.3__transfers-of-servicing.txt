## Transfers of Servicing


Effective 10/02/2019

The Seller's post-closing quality control program must include policies and procedures addressing Transfers of Servicing.

When Servicing is transferred, the Seller must:

- Retain copies of the file documents to complete the quality control reviews, or
- Make arrangements with the new Servicer to assist in the quality control reviews

Records of completed quality control reviews must be provided to the new Servicer upon request.

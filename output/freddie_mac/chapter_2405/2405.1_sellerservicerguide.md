# 2405.1: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/2405.1 -->

(a)

#### Process for requesting expense reimbursement and for receiving reimbursement of expenses

The Servicer must submit a request for reimbursement via PAID (Payments Automated Intelligent and Dynamic) (available at [https://sf.freddiemac.com/working-with-us/servicing/overview(opens in new window)](https://sf.freddiemac.com/working-with-us/servicing/overview)) in accordance with the requirements of this chapter:

- Any Servicer submitting an expense reimbursement request for the first time must obtain access to PAID through Access Manager
- Servicers may access the [PAID Online Help Section(opens in new window)](https://sf.freddiemac.com/faqs/paid-faq) for additional information and assistance with PAID

To be reimbursed for expenses, Servicers must authorize Freddie Mac to make such payments and receive debits via Automated Clearing House (ACH) credit entries into their commercial checking accounts.

Servicers must complete and execute [Form 1132](/app/guide/form/1132), in order to:

- Authorize receipt of ACH credit entries; and/or
- Update previously submitted ACH credit account instructions

[Form 1132](/app/guide/form/1132) must be submitted to Freddie Mac as an Electronic Record (as defined in [Section 1401.1(b)](/app/guide/section/1401.1#b)), using a Portable Document Format (PDF) (or other Electronic Record format commonly used in the mortgage industry), that has been completed and contains the copy or representation of the pen and ink signature of the Authorized Employee (such copy or representation of the Authorized Employee’s signature shall be such Authorized Employee’s adopted Electronic Signature as defined in [Section 1401.1(b)](/app/guide/section/1401.1#b).) Servicers may submit the completed form by either:

- **Email:** Attach to an e-mail and deliver to Freddie Mac at [CashCollections@FreddieMac.com](mailto:CashCollections@FreddieMac.com); or
- **Upload:** Submit through the Freddie Mac eBill system

Servicers may submit ACH credit-related questions or concerns to [Expense@FreddieMac.com](mailto:Expense@FreddieMac.com).

The employee authorized to execute [Form 1132](/app/guide/form/1132) on the Servicer’s behalf must be designated as an “Authorized Employee” on the Servicer’s applicable certificate of incumbency form:

- [Form 988SF](/app/guide/form/988SF); or
- [Form 989SF](/app/guide/form/989SF)

Before a Servicer may receive expense reimbursements and incentive payments via ACH credit entries, the Servicer must have completed the certificate of incumbency (“COI”) process requirements in [Section 2201.1](2201.1), and Freddie Mac must have accepted the Servicer’s COI and all other required forms and documents.

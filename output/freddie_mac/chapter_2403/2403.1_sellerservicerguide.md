# 2403.1: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/2403.1 -->

(a)

#### Sale of Mortgages through Loan Selling Advisor®

This chapter sets forth requirements for the sale of Mortgages through Loan Selling Advisor®.

By using Loan Selling Advisor, the Seller agrees to be bound by the Purchase Documents including, but not limited to, [Chapter 1401](/app/guide/chapter/1401), the applicable terms of this chapter, the terms of any applicable Guide chapters, and any other relevant Purchase Documents.

This Section 2403.1(a) is in addition to, and not in limitation of, [Chapter 2401](/app/guide/chapter/2401) and [Section 2402.2(e)](/app/guide/section/2402.2#e), which address the license terms applicable to the Sellers’ and Selling Agents’ use of Loan Selling Advisor in connection with the sale of Mortgages to Freddie Mac.

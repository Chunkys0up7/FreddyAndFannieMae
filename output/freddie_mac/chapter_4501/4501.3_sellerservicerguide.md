# 4501.3: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/4501.3 -->

(a)

#### Eligible property

A Home Possible® Mortgage must be secured by a 1- to 4-unit Primary Residence.

Home Possible Mortgages secured by Manufactured Homes must comply with all requirements of [Chapter 5703](/app/guide/chapter/5703) and this chapter.

Mortgages secured by a CHOICEHome® Manufactured Home must comply with the requirements of [Section 5703.12](5703.12) and this chapter.

Home Possible Mortgages may be secured by a Cooperative Unit if permitted under the Seller’s Purchase Documents and must comply with the requirements of [Chapter 5705](/app/guide/chapter/5705).

# 1401.3: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/1401.3 -->

(a)

#### Electronic Records and Electronic Signatures

Without limiting the definition of an Electronic Record set forth above, the term includes, by way of illustration only:

- A facsimile (“fax”) machine copy of a paper Record
- A fax machine copy of an Electronic Record
- An e-mail
- An e-mail with Electronic Record(s) attached
- A scanned copy of a paper Record or Electronic Record
- A paper Record converted into an Electronic Record

Unless otherwise provided in the Guide or Seller/Servicer’s other Purchase Documents, an Electronic Signature includes an Electronic copy or representation of a written signature or other Electronic signing process (permitted or required by Freddie Mac) that is attached to or logically associated with a paper Record or Electronic Record.

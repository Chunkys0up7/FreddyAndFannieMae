# 4406.5: Seller/ServicerGuide

<!-- chunk: freddie_mac/4406.5 -->

#### Value when resale restrictions survive foreclosure or recordation of a deed-in-lieu of foreclosure

For Mortgages secured by properties subject to resale restrictions (including income-based resale restrictions) that survive foreclosure or recordation of a deed-in-lieu of foreclosure, the value used to determine the loan-to-value (LTV)/total LTV (TLTV)/Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC) TLTV (HTLTV) ratio is as follows:

| Resale restrictions survive foreclosure or recordation of a deed-in-lieu of foreclosure | |
| --- | --- |
| Transaction type | Value |
| Purchase | Lesser of:   - Appraised value1 - Purchase price |
| Refinance | Appraised value1 |

1The appraisal report must reflect the impact the resale restrictions have on the subject property's value in accordance with [Section 4406.4(b)](/app/guide/section/4406.4#b).

Note: Automated collateral evaluation (ACE) or ACE+ PDR may only be accepted when the resale restrictions are age-based. Refer to [Sections 5602.3](5602.3) and [5602.4](5602.4), as applicable.
